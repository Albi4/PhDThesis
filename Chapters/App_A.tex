\section{Eddy Current suppression}
\label{Sec:EddyCurrents}

Stability and fast ramps are not the only problem. There is a lot of metal around the coils which gives origin to eddy currents Eddy currents slow downs the magnetic filed ramp and are originated from closed loops of metal. one of the original solution to counter act these unwanted effect was took long before my time in the experiment, and consisted in cutting one of the breadboard used to hold the optics in experiment like ours. these solution proved to be effective (find a fucking number), but remaining eddy currents from the metal vacuum chamber were still a limiting fact. 

A solution developed during the years (again starting before my time) was to measure the transfer function of our system and then convoluted the Laplace transform of the transfer function with the magnetic field ramp required in order to compensate the field. \section{A digital PID for fast magnetic field control}
\label{Sec:FPGA}


Final step was to combined what learned from having a fast power supply with a digital \ac{PID} to reduce even more imprecise ramps. The large power supply used to control the main coils is limited in resolution to perform small magnetic field ramps necessary to handle narrow magnetic Feshbach resonance. Our solution was to use the large coils to provide an off-set field and use a smaller power supply controlled with an home-made digital \ac{PID} based on a \ac{FPGA}, taking from an idea reported in Ref.~\cite{Thomas2020adf}, to do smaller magnetic field ramps.
