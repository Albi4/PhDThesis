\chapter{Additional measurements on the D\lowercase{y}-K Feshbach spectrum}
\label{Cpt:AddDyKRes}

All of the main results presented in this thesis are obtained working around the interspecies Feshbach resonance close to \SI{7}G. While this resonance proved to be a very fruitful tool to study interactions and Feshbach molecules, we also explored the interaction spectrum in the mixture at different magnetic fields, with the goal and hope to find other, possibly broader, candidates. In Section \ref{Sec:Compendium}, we explore the interaction spectrum over a large range of magnetic field and, in Section \ref{Sec:RF217}, we present an attempt to  better characterize the resonance at \SI{217}{G}.



\section{Compendium of  Dy-K Feshbach resonance}
\label{Sec:Compendium}



We individuate 34 interspecies Feshbach resonances across a magnetic field range of \SI{360}G using a field resolution of around \SI{200}{mG}.This choice ot magnetic field resolution is accordingly to the objective of looking for resonances broader than the one at \SI{7.3}{G}. This coarse scan thus probably missed different narrower features.


 In the end this scan proved to be without a real success no other feature broader then the one at \SI{217}{G} was found, except for a possible candidate around \SI{340}{G} which appeared to have a similar strength.
