\chapter{Additional measurements on the D\lowercase{y}-K Feshbach spectrum}
\label{Cpt:AddDyKRes}

All of the main results I present in this thesis are obtained working around the interspecies Feshbach resonance close to \SI{7}G. While this resonance proved to be a very fruitful tool to study interactions in the mixture, we also explored, at different times, the interaction spectrum over a large range, with the hope of finding other possible candidates. In this chapter, I present the result of this investigation. As previously, we look for maxima of thermalization between the two species as indication of high scattering rates.
