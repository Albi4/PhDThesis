\chapter{Additional measurements on the D\lowercase{y}-K Feshbach spectrum}
\label{Cpt:AddDyKRes}

All of the main results I present in this thesis are obtained working around the interspecies Feshbach resonance close to \SI{7}G. While this resonance proved to be a very fruitful tool to study interactions in the mixture, we also explored the interaction spectrum in the mixture over a large range of magnetic field, with the goal of finding other possible candidates. In this chapter, I briefly summarize the result of this investigation. As previously, we look for maxima of thermalization between the two species as indication of high scattering rates. 

We individuate \albi{xx} interspecies Feshbach resonances across \SI{360}G using a magnetic field resolution of \SI{180}{mG}.\albi{true above 170 G, check how much is elsewhere} The choice ot magnetic field resolution is accordingly to the idea of looking for resonances broader than the one at \SI{7.3}{G}.
