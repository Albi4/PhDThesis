, mainly by combining two different alkali metals, as a step with which assemble chemically stable ground state molecules. 


This branch of research, pioneered by with the study of KRb at JILA and in the last few years has reached great success with the realization of degenerate Fermi gas of NaK molecules \cite{Schindewolf2022eom} and with the successful production of a \ac{mBEC} with NaCs \cite{Bigagli2024oob} and NaRb \cite{Shi2025bec}. Even more recently there has been a flourishing of proposal to study unusual ground state molecules for precision measurements \cite{Ciamei2026uhs}.
