\chapter{What have we learned?}
\label{Chpt:Summary}

In this chapter we summarize the main lesson learned from the Dy-K experiment, both from a technical and conceptual standpoint, while next chapter instead will provide a broader view for future attempts at studying mass imbalance Fermi gases and in general unusual quantum mixtures.


Evaporative cooling in the Dy-K mixture has been found to be mostly effective for low magnetic field, in a region below \SI{500}{mG}. 



Over reliance on the magnetic field gradient: great tool, that revealed to be also a great limitation.


Metallic chamber waay too big and adding too many eddy currents

Poor imaging, fine for what was the goal up to now, but since early days one of the main limiting factors. a long transport towards a different chamber could be challenging for the wavelengths available a full glass cell solution, like the one implemented in the NewlanD experiment of Dr. Emili Kirilov in our group, could be the best platform.

Fermionic dysprosium properties of interactions not yet know as much as necessary and some studies are already currently been worked in the laboratory to fill some of this gaps
